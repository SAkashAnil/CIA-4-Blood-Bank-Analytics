How each indicator is defined
Units in stock
Units that have been collected and not yet issued, expired or discarded.
Units at expiry risk
In-stock units that expire within their risk window: red cells 7 days, platelets 48 hours, plasma 30 days.
Wastage rate
Expired units divided by units collected in the selected period.
Fulfilment rate
Requests met in full divided by all requests in the selected period.
Emergency response time
Average minutes from request to fulfilment for emergency-urgency requests.
Unmet requests
Requests that were only partly met or not met at all, a proxy for stockout incidents.
Repeat donor rate
Donors, after removing duplicate records, who donated more than once, divided by all donors.
Targets
Wastage, fulfilment and response targets shown on the cards are illustrative management assumptions.
